# 🧭 CivicBridge AI

### Don't know where to start? Just tell us what happened.

CivicBridge AI is a Gemini-powered universal action navigator that converts messy real-world situations into clear, prioritized and verifiable next steps.

## 🚀 What it does

Users can explain a situation naturally or upload a document.

CivicBridge uses Gemini to identify:

- 🧠 What the user means
- 📌 Important facts
- 🟡 Priority
- ❓ Missing information
- ✅ Next actions
- 📋 Checklist
- 🔴 Information that needs verification

## 💡 Why CivicBridge?

People don't speak in forms, APIs or government terminology.

They speak in messy human language.

CivicBridge acts as a bridge between **human intent and complex systems**, helping people understand what to do next without needing to understand the system first.

## ✨ Key Features

- Natural-language input
- Gemini-powered reasoning
- Structured JSON output
- Priority detection
- Missing-information detection
- Action checklist
- Verification warnings
- Document/image upload support
- Simple action-focused interface
